How ResQNet works

One incident. Four connected stages.

Every stage adds context until an authorized responder has a clearer picture of what needs attention.

01

Capture

Receive citizen, responder and authorized information-channel reports in a common incident format.

02

Examine

Assist credibility triage using source context, corroboration, timestamps and location consistency.

03

Locate

Put incidents, resources and response teams on the same geographic picture.

04

Coordinate

Explore resources and potential cascading effects, then support human decisions.
