The idea

What is ResQNet?

A simple way to explain the product: turn scattered crisis signals into a trusted, location-aware response workspace.

During a disaster, information arrives from many places: citizens, field responders, public-information channels, hospitals, ambulances, maps and other connected systems. ResQNet is designed to connect those signals into one workflow. Incoming reports can be examined for credibility, placed on a live map, matched with nearby resources and response teams, and used to identify possible cascading impacts such as road access, medical demand or shelter pressure. An AI Copilot then gives authorized users a conversational way to summarize the situation, compare available resources and explore what may need verification next. The AI supports human decision-makers; it does not autonomously make life-critical decisions.

Why it matters

From one report to the bigger picture.

ResQNet is designed around the reality that disasters are connected systems, not isolated incidents.
